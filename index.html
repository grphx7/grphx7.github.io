Welcome to the Real-Time Gas Earner. I created this web app to visualize the grim reality of converting labor directly into fossil fuels. By inputting your wage and local gas prices, you can watch in real-time as your hard work slowly trickles into a gas jug. In today's economy, where the cost of commuting eats into the wages earned *from* that commute, this tool serves as a satirical, geographic calculator of your daily economic grind.
